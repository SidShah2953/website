                        A second, independent count from the Cloudflare beacon. It counts automated traffic GoatCounter filters out, so its numbers run several times higher. Compare trends, not totals.

                          About these numbers

                          • Visitors are GoatCounter's: one per person per page per day, bots excluded. No cookies, nothing stored on the reader's device, so there is no way to recognise a returning reader, and nothing here identifies anyone except subscribers, who gave their email.
                          • Engaged time counts only while the tab is visible and the reader has scrolled, moved, touched or typed in the last 30 seconds. Averages are estimates from the marks reached (15s, 30s, 1m, 2m, 5m, 10m, 20m), so they slightly understate.
                          • Read means the reader reached 90% of the body and spent at least 35% of its estimated reading time.
                          • Signup rate is signups divided by visitors who actually had the form on screen.
                          • Engaged time, signup views and signups, video, PDF and outbound clicks are tracked from 11 Oct 2026; scroll depth and reads from earlier. Newsletter clicks arrive tagged utm_source=newsletter.
                          • Everything is cached in a database and synced every 30 minutes, so the page never waits on an outside service.
